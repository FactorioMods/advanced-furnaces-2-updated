# Settings and compatibility

## Furnaces and research

Crafting speed, energy consumption and pollution are configurable at startup. Research profiles include original, vanilla and Space Age progression. Missing expansion science falls back to an available profile. Changing a profile does not reset completed research.

The steel-yield setting controls the existing five-iron-ore recipe, which produces one steel by default.

## Additional ores

Additional ore smelting is disabled by default. When enabled, it uses suitable ore-to-plate recipes from installed mods and can combine alternate ore-to-iron and iron-to-steel recipes into a direct steel recipe. Ore quantities and total crafting time are preserved; source research and the first advanced furnace are required.

Automatic recipe generation excludes fluids, random or fractional results, byproducts, special quality/freshness rules, surface restrictions, recycling and chemical processes. This option does not provide general foundry or overhaul compatibility.

## Conveyors and loaders

Bundled loaders match their belt tier's speed and graphics. Wagon loading is disabled by default. Automatic-only mode handles trains stopped at a station; all-trains mode also handles stopped manual trains. Wagon layouts must be cardinal, not diagonal.

Loaders support quality-aware whitelist and blacklist filters. PRO belts were also checked with Miniloader Redux Fork 1.0.5.

## Mod combinations

Base and Space Age settings were tested. Hooks for Angels, Bob's, Pyanodon, Bio Industries, Industrial Revolution and Space Exploration remain, but complete overhaul campaigns have not been validated. SE and combined SE/Space Age research profiles are experimental.

Enable only one Advanced Furnaces derivative. Multiplayer peers need matching versions and startup settings.
